# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation must remain respectful, evidence-based, and focused on claims,
methods, software, and reproducibility rather than individuals. Examples of
expected behavior include:

- disclosing relevant conflicts of interest;
- protecting confidential reviewer or contributor material;
- distinguishing blocking scientific concerns from preferences;
- citing prior work accurately;
- correcting errors transparently;
- welcoming technically grounded disagreement.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, plagiarism,
retaliation, coercive authorship or affiliation practices, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, exposure of private information, and sustained
personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `research@asr-lab.online`. Reports
will be reviewed confidentially and addressed proportionately. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
