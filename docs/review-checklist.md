# Review checklist

- [ ] Research question and estimand are explicit.
- [ ] Cash, premium, interest, and liquidation conventions are explicit.
- [ ] Policy information sets are matched in comparisons.
- [ ] Cost-aware non-neural benchmarks are included.
- [ ] Results use multiple training seeds.
- [ ] Paired confidence intervals are reported.
- [ ] Heston discretization has a substep sensitivity analysis.
- [ ] In-distribution adaptation is not called robustness.
- [ ] Percentage CVaR changes identify their cash origin.
- [ ] Every table and figure is generated from archived machine-readable data.
- [ ] Claims distinguish partial replication from full reproduction.
