\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

The study sits at the intersection of incomplete-market hedging, coherent risk
measurement, and neural stochastic control. Classical option replication under
continuous frictionless trading follows \citet{blackscholes1973} and
\citet{merton1973}. Once proportional transaction costs or discrete trading are
introduced, perfect replication generally fails and optimal policies may be
characterized by asymptotic corrections, stochastic control, or variational
inequalities \citep{leland1985,hoggardwhalleywilmott1994,whalleywilmott1997}.
The present work does not claim that neural networks replace these analytical
methods; rather, they provide a flexible policy class when the objective,
frictions, and scenario generator are specified numerically.

The risk criterion is Expected Shortfall, also called Conditional
Value-at-Risk. Its optimization representation is due to
\citet{rockafellaruryasev2000}; the coherent-risk perspective is developed by
\citet{artzner1999} and the convex-risk framework by
\citet{follmerschied2002}. The finite-batch distinction made in this paper is
important because profiling the empirical threshold solves the batch problem
exactly but does not remove sampling error relative to population CVaR.

Neural approaches to derivative pricing and hedging predate modern deep
learning \citep{hutchinson1994}. \citet{buehler2019} formulate Deep Hedging as
direct optimization of a convex risk functional over neural trading
strategies. Related reinforcement-learning formulations include
\citet{kolmritter2019}, \citet{halperin2020}, and \citet{cao2021}. The current
paper differs from these works by emphasizing auditability: a compact NumPy
implementation, explicit pathwise derivatives, exact empirical-tail weights,
and a transparent account of which parts of the original experiment are not
reproduced.

Stochastic-volatility simulations use the Heston model \citep{heston1993} and
a full-truncation Euler discretization following \citet{lord2010}. Because the
Feller condition need not hold for the selected stress parameters, the
repository includes substep diagnostics and treats Heston comparisons as
simulation-dependent rather than model-free evidence.
